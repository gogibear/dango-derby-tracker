## Assumptions

If product changes these, re-check the whole doc.

- iOS app, US-only, 18+, real-money mini golf. Deposit to play for money; free play always available.
- Paid opponents are ghosts (recorded runs) of verified real humans. ⚠ Confirm final format: game doc says ~8-player Grand Prix, launch issue #222 says you + 5.
- Anyone can play a course free in the browser via a shared challenge link; money play is app-only.
- Off-chain: no crypto, no wallet — just an app you win cash in. Deposits/payouts run through the credits platform's cash rails. ⚠ Confirm payout speed with Tarrence (not crypto-instant anymore).
- Live domain is play.dangoderby.com. "PuttFall" is the old internal name, same game.
- Launch target: ~Sept 1.

## Positioning

The cutest game you can win real money in. Race real people. Trust line (for the deposit screen and press, not the ad hook): every opponent is a verified real human, and we can prove it. No crypto, no wallet — a simpler pitch than anything crypto-flavored. ⚠ Confirm payout speed — if withdrawals land fast, "cash out fast" moves up into the hook; if not, don't claim it.

## Rules

1. Every public claim comes from the approved-claims list. No earnings promises. Never imply live head-to-head play.
2. Everything money-facing carries 18+ and the eligible-states line. Every paid or creator post carries clear #ad disclosure.
3. Promo money (freerolls, bonus credits, prize-pool entries) never mixes with real money in any metric.
4. No scaling an ad until it profitably converts deposits. Winners scale, everything else dies.

## 1 · Foundations — accounts, brand, people, tools

- [ ] Social accounts created, branded, warming: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, X
- [ ] Brand kit final: logo, character renders, one-pager, boilerplate — plus the machine-readable brand folder so our AI tools generate on-brand
- [ ] Experienced real-money-gaming marketer(s) retained to walk us through paid + creators (possibly one per platform)
- [ ] Team can operate the stack: AppsFlyer (tells us which ad or creator caused each install), the ad platforms, Okara (AI agents that produce SEO/social content), marketing-engine (generates ad creative)
- [ ] Player trust live: support inbox someone owns, a defined path for "I can't withdraw," responsible-gaming page on the site

## 2 · Where players land — store, site, challenge link

- [ ] App Store listing done: name, subtitle, screenshots, preview video. Story: cute → race real people → win real money → trust
- [ ] Store keywords set: "mini golf," "cash games," "win real money" families
- [ ] play.dangoderby.com live: play-a-course-right-now button up top, App Store badge, email waitlist, press kit
- [ ] Challenge-link loop works end to end: share link → friend plays that exact course in the browser in seconds → "get the app to play for money" → install attributed
- [ ] Stranger test passed: 5 people who've never seen it — value clear in 30 seconds, playing inside 60

## 3 · Creators — borrowed audiences, our first real traffic

- [ ] 3–5 anchor creators signed (golf gaming, skill-cash reviewers, adult-skew audiences), given early access; their recorded runs double as launch ghost records
- [ ] Volume layer flowing: 10–15 smaller creators via Sideshift (a marketplace where you order UGC videos), with tight quality control
- [ ] One creator brief: what the game is, example posts, their personal challenge link, pay terms, required disclosures, banned claims
- [ ] Reuse rights secured in every deal, so winning creator videos can run as our paid ads through the creator's own account ("whitelisting" / Spark Ads)
- [ ] Creator tracker live: contacted → signed → posted, cost, installs and deposits per creator
- [ ] Launch-day posting schedule locked, calendar invites sent

## 4 · Paid ads — buys the eyes we don't have

- [ ] Real-money-gaming advertiser approval filed with TikTok, Meta, AppLovin (needs the legal opinion + entity docs); status tracked per network; spend starts wherever clears first
- [ ] AppsFlyer installed and correctly attributing test installs on each network
- [ ] First creative batch ready: AI-generated ads + best creator clips
- [ ] Test plan written before any spend: $10–25k, 3–5 concepts per network, kill or scale on cost-per-deposit — never clicks or installs
- [ ] Weekly readout running: spend, cost per deposit, what died, what scaled

## 5 · Organic content — the content factory

Job: keep the accounts alive and feed paid with candidates. On a cold start, reach is a bonus, not the plan.

- [ ] Content calendar + tracker running: plan Monday, produce midweek, post, review Friday
- [ ] Launch-week bank scheduled: 30+ videos, made native per platform (no watermarked cross-posts)
- [ ] First formats defined: "beat this ghost" duels, near-miss heartbreaks, photo finishes, cash-out moments (inside claims rules), course/skin spotlights
- [ ] Every post ends at a challenge link
- [ ] Account architecture decided: one brand account vs several

## 6 · Analytics — the scoreboard (Kariy)

- [ ] Full funnel measured on web + iOS: install → signup → first free run → deposit → first paid entry → cash-out
- [ ] Every creator and every challenge link individually trackable
- [ ] One dashboard everyone reads; Friday readout
- [ ] Promo-vs-cash separation built into the numbers themselves
- [ ] LTV model v1 scheduled (~2 weeks after launch data starts) — sets creator pay rates and the "safe to spend more" bar

## 7 · Compliance — stay inside the lines (get outside help here)

- [ ] Approved-claims list (+ banned phrases) ratified with counsel before the first creative ships
- [ ] Platform rules read once, written down, shared with the team: TikTok, Meta, YouTube, X each treat money games differently
- [ ] Creator contract covers disclosure, reuse rights, geography, claims
- [ ] Eligible-states list drives every ad target, creator callout, and line of copy

## 8 · Launch day — one day, everything fires

- [ ] Launch-week prize pool on the free ladder: size + terms signed off (this is the news)
- [ ] Launch video: under 2 minutes, product in the first 3 seconds
- [ ] Announcement wave committed: X thread + ~50 friendly accounts (investors, friends, creators) amplifying in the first 30 minutes. X is the industry megaphone; players come from TikTok creators + the prize pool
- [ ] Waitlist email drafted: what launched, one link
- [ ] Date picked; hour-by-hour runbook + 48-hour follow-up written

## Not doing for launch — decided, don't reopen

Referral program (post-launch; spec exists) · Derby Day streamer bracket · brand partnerships · community programs · lifecycle/re-engagement emails · Android.
